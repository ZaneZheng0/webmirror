# Authorized Use

WebMirror is an archival and development tool. Technical access to a page does
not grant legal permission to copy, modify, publish, or redistribute it.

Use WebMirror only when at least one of the following is true:

- You own the site and its content.
- The site owner has explicitly authorized the capture and intended use.
- A license clearly permits the capture, modification, and distribution you
  plan to perform.
- Applicable law permits the specific use after appropriate legal review.

Do not use WebMirror to bypass authentication, paywalls, access controls, rate
limits, DRM, or technical restrictions. Do not capture private accounts,
personal data, confidential material, or regulated information without a
documented lawful basis and authorization.

Before capturing:

1. Confirm the page, assets, fonts, media, trademarks, and code are covered by
   your permission.
2. Confirm whether the result may be kept privately, shared internally, or
   redistributed publicly.
3. Review the source site's terms, contractual restrictions, and applicable
   privacy obligations.
4. Avoid pages containing secrets, account data, or user-submitted personal
   information.
5. Use a conservative capture rate and stop if the site reports errors or asks
   you to stop.

After capturing:

- Review `mirror.json` and the validation report for missing or online
  dependencies.
- Do not present a mirror as the original operator's official site.
- Preserve required copyright, license, and attribution notices.
- Delete the output when the authorization or retention purpose ends.

WebMirror's filtering, redaction, and access-control checks reduce accidental
exposure. They do not determine ownership, license rights, privacy compliance,
or whether a particular use is lawful.
